## Points to keep

- The Planet restriction affects that optional dataset, not OSM roads.
- Microsoft's `LICENSE` file reads "CLDA Permissive 2.0", while its README states CDLA Permissive 2.0 with a link to https://cdla.dev/permissive-2-0/. The other channel, Planetary Computer, shows ODbL-1.0.
- Keeping layers separate does not automatically settle licensing duties. Whether something counts as a derivative or collective database depends on how data is combined, and this needs legal review.
- Kaggle labels are uploader claims. The Techsalerator bundle's Apache label sits on another vendor's sample, and the population copy's CC0 label conflicts with HDX's CC BY.
- Attribution lines need collecting per source before any output is shared.

## Data Privacy Act

RA 10173 applies to personal information of Philippine residents, and data-sharing arrangements call for safeguards (secondary summaries read). The IRR's profiling definition lists location among evaluated aspects, per a secondary summary. Whether aggregated or anonymised mobile location data falls outside the Act: **Not found**, and treated as unresolved. Any phone-derived foot-traffic data, and any company sales data, needs a privacy review before use.
